## Out-of-hours rule

**Rule:** Queue + auto-reply. No live routing after 18:00 IST.

Auto-reply text (sent by Claude via WA / IG DM):
> "Thanks for reaching out to Team Empire 👋 Our team is available 9am–6pm IST,
> Mon–Sat. We'll get back to you first thing on the next business day!"

Lead is persisted to the DB as normal. Slack notification is **suppressed** outside
business hours — the channel ping fires when the team comes online at 09:00 IST
the following business day (requires scheduler support in v0.6.x; not in v0.5).

> ⚠️ Time-gated Slack pings require `notify_lead_arrived()` to accept a
> `schedule_at` param. Mark as v0.6.x backlog if not in v0.6.0 scope.

## Escalation (SLA breach)

If a lead has **no response claim within the SLA window**:

1. Auto-DM **Mano K M** (`U0AMJBY5ERX`) with:
   - Lead name / identifier
   - Category + product
   - Time elapsed since inbound
   - Assigned channel link
2. **Do not auto-reassign** — two people working the same lead is worse than a delay.
3. Mano decides: hold, reassign, or escalate to CEO.

> Requires escalation scheduler in v0.6.x. For v0.5 launch, SLA breach tracking
> is manual — Mano monitors #leads-* channels.
